    Problems#

    1. Maximum Modulo Product#

    Given:

    (x mod a) * (y mod b)

    Read the above as “(x mod a) times (y mod b)”.

    Assume x, y, a, and b are all positive integers.

    Question: What is the largest value this product can have?

    Write the answer in terms of x, y, a, and b.

    2. Alien World Digital Clock#

    On the planet Zorax, time is divided differently from Earth.

    A Zoraxian digital clock follows these rules:

    • 1 Zoraxian minute = 80 seconds
    • 1 Zoraxian hour = 75 Zoraxian minutes
    • 1 Zoraxian day = 20 Zoraxian hours

    The clock displays time as:

    HH : MM : SS

    where:

    • HH is the hour
    • MM is the minute
    • SS is the number of seconds into the current minute

    At the beginning of each day, the clock shows:

    00 : 00 : 00

    Example#

    If 5,432 seconds have elapsed since the beginning of the day, the clock shows:

    00 : 67 : 72

    because 5,432 seconds is 67 Zoraxian minutes and 72 seconds.

    Your Turn#

    If 98,765 seconds have elapsed since the beginning of the day, what will the digital clock show?

    Use modulo, floor, and ceiling operations where appropriate to determine the hour, minute, and second.
