    Expression Trees#

    Task#

    For each of the following expressions:

    1. Draw the expression tree.
    2. Trace the expression tree and show the order of evaluation.
    3. Calculate and write the final result.

    Basic Arithmetic Expressions#

    • 5 + 3 * 2

    • (6 * 7) + 3

    • 8 + 3 * (6 - 2)

    • (15 - 4) * 3 + 18 / 6

    • 7 * (5 + 2) - 3 ^ 2

    • (24 / (3 + 1)) + 5 * 2

    • 18 - (4 * (3 + 2)) + 2 ^ 3

    • (6 + 4) * (9 - 3) / 2

    • 25 % (4 + 3) * 2 + 5

    • ((8 + 2) * 3) - (5 ^ 2 / 5)

    • 4 ^ 2 + (18 / (3 * 2)) - 7

    • (20 - (3 + 2) * 2) ^ 2 / 5

    • ((7 + 5) * (8 - 3)) / (2 + 3)

    • 3 ^ (2 + 1) - ((16 / 4) * (7 - 2))

    • ((24 / (2 + 4)) + (5 * (3 ^ 2))) - 8

    • ((18 - (2 ^ 3)) * (4 + 1)) / (15 % 4)

    • (((5 + 3) ^ 2) - (6 * (4 + 2))) / (7 - 3)

    Logical Expressions#

    • 8 > 5 && 12 < 20

    • 10 == 10 || 7 > 15

    • (10 > 5 && 6 < 9) || 3 == 4

    • 15 > 10 && (4 < 5 || 8 < 6)

    • (7 < 10 || 12 == 12) && 5 > 2

    • 20 > 15 && (8 < 5 || 6 == 6)

    • (4 == 4 && 9 > 3) || (7 < 5 && 2 > 1)

    • (15 > 10 || 3 == 8) && (6 > 6 || 4 > 4)

    • ((8 > 5 && 12 < 20) || 7 == 9) && 15 > 1

    Funtional Expressions#

    • add(5, 3)

    • mul(add(5, 3), 4)

    • sub(20, mul(3, 4))

    • isEqual(add(5, 3), 8)

    • max(add(4, 6), mul(2, 5))

    • min(sub(20, 7), add(4, 5))

    • isEqual(mul(3, add(2, 4)), 18)

    • max(div(20, 4), sub(15, 6))

    • isEqual(mod(mul(7, 3), 5), 1)

    • add(mul(3, 4), sub(10, 6))

    • max(add(5, 3), min(10, mul(2, 4)))

    • isEqual( add(mul(3, 4), sub(10, 6)), max(10, mul(2, 7)) )

    • min( max(add(4, 5), mul(2, 6)), sub(30, div(20, 4)) )

    • isEqual( max( add(5, mul(2, 3)), sub(20, div(16, 4)) ), min( mul(3, 6), add(10, mod(17, 5)) ) )

    • isEqual( add( mul(3, 4), div(20, 5) ), max( sub(20, 2), add(7, 3) ) )
