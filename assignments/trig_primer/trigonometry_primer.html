Radians

Degrees divide a turn into 360 equal pieces. Radians measure the angle using the circle itself: the angle is the length of the arc divided by the radius.

A complete circumference is 2πr. For a full turn, arc ÷ radius is therefore 2π.
Notice
Radians are not a second kind of angle. They are a different way of measuring the same angle.

08

The Relationship Between Sine and Cosine

The two functions are not independent. They come from the same point on the same circle, so their values must obey the geometry of that circle.

A geometric constraint

On the unit circle, every point is exactly one unit from the origin. By the Pythagorean theorem:

cos² θ + sin² θ = 1

Change the angle and the two values change, but their squared lengths always add to 1.

Notice
The equation is not a mysterious trig rule. It is the equation of the circle.
