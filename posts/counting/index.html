    Counting, Numbers, and More#

    Quiz#

    We started the session with this quiz on an earlier topic.

    Counting#

    We explored the basics of counting, starting with a simple question: what is a digit?

    We then considered how many numbers can be represented using ten fingers. This led to a thought experiment: if a crow with six fingers joined the count, how many numbers could we represent?

    From there, the discussion introduced the idea of placeholder counting and how a count can be represented using binary.

    Representation Using Different Bases#

    We looked at representing numbers using different bases. In particular, we covered how to convert a number from base 10 to another base less than 10.

    How to Remember Anything#

    We discussed spaced repetition as a way to remember information more effectively.

    A useful resource mentioned during the session was Ncase’s “How to Remember Anything”. We also discussed AnkiDroid as a tool for applying spaced repetition.

    As a practical goal, everyone was asked to use spaced repetition to learn the capitals of all 195 countries within the next month.

    Role Models#

    We discussed the importance of cultivating role models in your chosen profession.

    Some of the people mentioned as potential role models to look up were:

    • Steve Wozniak
    • Paul Allen
    • Terence Tao
    • Grace Hopper
    • Manjul Bhargava
    • Claude Shannon

    The idea was not simply to know their names, but to learn more about their work and careers.

    Number Systems#

    We continued with number systems and the number line.

    We discussed:

    • Integers — numbers that can be represented with denominator 1.
    • Rational numbers — numbers that can be expressed as a ratio of integers, with a non-zero integer denominator.
    • Irrational numbers — numbers that cannot be expressed as a ratio of integers.

    Modulo#

    We introduced the modulo operation, which gives the integer remainder after division.

    For the examples discussed, we assumed positive numbers. We also looked at the cyclical nature of modulo, where values repeat after reaching the modulus.

    Floor and Ceiling#

    We defined two operations involving integers:

    • Floor — the greatest integer less than or equal to a given number.
    • Ceiling — the smallest integer greater than or equal to a given number.

    An Unexpected Discussion About Cars#

    Someone had worn a Porsche shirt, which led to a broader discussion about cars.

    We wondered why so many Italian carmakers are associated with high-performance cars, while the American automotive industry has historically taken a different direction.

    This led to a discussion about Ford, the large distances between places in the United States, and how these geographical factors contributed to the importance of private cars in America.

    We contrasted this with Europe, where public transportation is generally much more accessible between cities, reducing the need for everyone to depend on a private car.

    We also discussed the geography of Europe and how Hitler’s interest in mass motorisation and the Volkswagen project, which was influenced in part by ideas associated with Ford’s approach to automobile production, helped push the development of the Volkswagen.
