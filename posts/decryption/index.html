    History#

    • Why this activity?
    • A brief history of computing
    • Entscheidungsproblem by David Hilbert
    • Kurt Gödel publishes his incompleteness theorems
    • Inspired by all this Alan Turing proves that a general solution to Hilbert’s question is impossible and proceeds to find a limit to what can be computed. In doing so he invents the Turing Machine which is what modern computers are primarily based on.
    • Turing gets enlisted by the British government to work in Bletchley Park on the Engima project
    • Germans use the Engima machine to encrypt messages and send to their troops. This proves very hard to crack
    • With some luck and ingenuity - and the clever use of a computer - Alan Turing solves this problem, thus setting off the modern computer age.
