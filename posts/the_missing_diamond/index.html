    🔎 The Missing Diamond#

    The Case#

    At 8:00 PM, the famous Blue Star Diamond disappeared from a locked display room at the City Museum.

    There were only three people inside the museum at the time:

    • Arun — Security Guard

    • Meera — Museum Assistant

    • Ravi — Electrician

    The police arrived immediately.

    The display room had no broken windows or damaged locks.

    🕵️ Clue 1 — The Security Camera#

    The camera showed that the display room was opened at 7:42 PM.

    The museum assistant, Meera, had the access card needed to open the room.

    But there was one problem:

    The access card was used while Meera was standing in the cafeteria.

    So, did Meera steal the diamond?

    🔦 Clue 2 — The Power Cut#

    At 7:40 PM, the museum experienced a short power failure.

    The electrician, Ravi, was working near the electrical control room.

    The security guard said:

    “During the power cut, I went downstairs to check the main switch.”

    So both Ravi and Arun had a reason to be near the electrical room.

    But the diamond could only be removed if:

    The display room was opened and the security alarm was disabled.

    🧩 Clue 3 — The Alarm#

    Police discovered that the alarm was disabled at 7:41 PM.

    Only Arun’s security code could disable it.

    However…

    Arun’s code was entered from inside the security office.

    And Arun’s fingerprint was not found on the security-office keyboard.

    ☕ Clue 4 — The Unexpected Detail#

    A coffee cup was found beside the security-office computer.

    It had Meera’s fingerprint on it.

    Meera immediately said:

    “Yes, I had coffee there earlier. But I didn’t enter the security code.”

    The investigators now had three possibilities.
