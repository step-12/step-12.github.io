      Intro to Git/GitHub

      September 25, 2026
      Git, Gh, Repo Creation, Gacp

      So far, whatever the raylib code you wrote has stored on your computer.

      Now think about these situations:

      • What if your laptop suddenly stops working?
      • What if you want your code reviewed by mentors or peers, may be during the weekend?
      • What if your laptop gets lost or damaged?
      • What if you want to access your code from another computer?
      • What if you are away from your laptop but need to look at your latest code?

      All of these point to the same problem: your code lives in exactly one place.
