    Habit Challenge Cards#

    Each card is a scenario. Your team designs the system that solves it, using the four laws — make it obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying. For the cards under Breaking a Bad Habit, invert them: make it invisible, unattractive, hard, and unsatisfying.

    Building a Good Habit#

    Daily Reading Habit#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who reads zero pages a week. Design a system that gets them into a sustainable daily reading habit.

    Fitness / Movement Habit#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who hasn’t exercised since college and dreads the idea of ‘working out.’ Design a system that gets them moving most days without relying on motivation.

    English Speaking / Public Communication#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who freezes up speaking in meetings or in English generally. Design a system that builds daily speaking reps into their week.

    Personal Finance / Budgeting#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who’s never tracked a rupee and is already living paycheck to paycheck. Design a system that gets them budgeting and saving automatically.

    Journaling / Reflection Habit#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who’s tried journaling five times and quit five times. Design a system that makes daily reflection stick.

    Consistent Sleep / Wake-Up Routine#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who sleeps at random hours and starts every day rushed. Design a system that locks in a consistent wake time.

    Skill-Building Habit#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who wants to learn a new skill ’eventually’ but never opens the course. Design a system that turns ’eventually’ into daily practice.

    Networking / Relationship-Building Habit#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who never reaches out to people unless they need something. Design a system for staying in touch and building their network as a habit, not a scramble.

    Breaking a Bad Habit#

    Sugar / Junk Food#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who reaches for sugar every time they’re stressed or bored. Design a system that breaks the reach, using the inverted four laws.

    Instagram / TikTok Doomscroll#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad losing 90+ minutes a night to the scroll. Design a system that makes the habit invisible, hard, and costly.

    Procrastination on Starting Tasks#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who always finds something else to do before starting real work. Design a system that kills the delay at the two-minute mark.

    Late-Night Screen Use / Poor Sleep Hygiene#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who’s on their phone in bed until 1am every night. Design a system that removes the cue instead of asking for willpower.

    Impulse Spending#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who buys on impulse every time an ad or sale notification hits. Design a system that adds friction and real cost to the impulse.

    Negative Self-Talk / Comparison Spiral#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who spirals into self-criticism after every small mistake or every scroll through someone else’s highlight reel. Design a system that interrupts the spiral before it takes hold.

    Notification-Driven Distraction at Work#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who can’t hold focus for 20 minutes without checking a ping. Design a system that makes deep, uninterrupted work the path of least resistance.

    Skipping Breakfast / Erratic Eating#

    Your team is handed a fresh grad who skips breakfast most days and crashes by 11am. Design a system that makes eating on schedule the easy default.
